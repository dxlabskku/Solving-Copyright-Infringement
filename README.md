# Solving Copyright Infringement on Short Video Platforms

> **Accepted at IJCAI 2025**  
> [arXiv:2504.21772](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21772)

---

## Overview

Short video platforms such as **YouTube Shorts** and **TikTok** often suffer from copyright issues, where users embed **arbitrary background music (BGM)** to obscure the **original soundtrack (OST)**.  
This not only disrupts content originality but also hinders the effectiveness of audio-based retrieval systems.

To address this, we propose a **deep learning pipeline** that integrates:
- **Music Source Separation (MSS)** to isolate OST and BGM
- **Cross-Modal Video-Music Retrieval (CMVMR)** to ensure the restored audio aligns with the original video content

We also introduce two **domain-specific datasets** to support training and evaluation:
- **OASD-20K**: 20,000 audio clips with mixed BGM and OST
- **OSVAR-160**: 1,121 video-audio pairs designed to benchmark BGM removal and OST restoration

---

## Key Contributions

- A **novel pipeline** combining MSS and CMVMR for short video audio restoration
- Two **publicly curated datasets** tailored for BGM separation and copyright research
